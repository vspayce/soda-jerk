# Soda Jerk — Backlog (post-prototype)

The classic level (this repo) comes first. Everything below is future
scope, captured so it doesn't get lost.

## Additional level types

- **Upside-down bar** — serving concretes (frozen custard). Needs a
  decision: is it visually inverted (customers/bar on the ceiling,
  you serve upward), or just re-themed but plays like the classic
  level? Open question from design chat, not yet resolved.
- **Sliding level** ("like the penguin slide game") — mechanic still
  TBD. Two candidates discussed: (a) tilt/steer a sliding object down
  an icy path avoiding obstacles, or (b) drinks slide down a ramp and
  you catch them at the bottom. Needs a follow-up design pass.
- **Carousel level** — you stand in the center and throw drinks
  outward to bars arranged in a star pattern around you. Aiming/
  targeting mechanic not yet designed.
- **Mini-games — soda jerk tricks** — bottle-flair/glass-trick style
  mini-games. Concept only, no mechanics defined yet.

## Audio

- Wurlitzer jukebox-style music for the classic level and menus.

## Props / theming

- Soda machines, siphons, burgers, shakes.
- Drinks in various colors with wild, period-appropriate names.

## Open questions to revisit

- How do the level types fit together structurally — level select,
  unlock progression, or do they stay separate experiments?
- Persistent high score / leaderboard — explicitly deferred for the
  prototype, revisit once the core loop is proven fun.
